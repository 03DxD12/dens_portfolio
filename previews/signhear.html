Accessible Science learning for every learner, every ability, and every classroom.

SIGNHEAR provides accessible Science lessons, visual learning materials, interactive quizzes, and Sign Language Alphabet Recognition to support inclusive learning in Philippine schools.

Features

🧏 💬

Sign Language Support

Each module includes sign language guides, visual diagrams, and interactive learning resources.

🎯 📚

Interactive Activities

Learning tasks help students practise and strengthen their understanding of Science.

✅ ⚙️

Interactive Quizzes

Assessments help students check their understanding and receive lesson feedback.

👩‍🏫 📊

Teacher Dashboard

Teachers can track progress and scores and upload inclusive learning materials.

☁️ 💾

Offline-Ready

Approved resources support classrooms with limited or intermittent connectivity.
